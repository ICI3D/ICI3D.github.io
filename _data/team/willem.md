bio: Lander Willem is Assistant Professor at theUniversity of Antwerp, specialising in data-driven health economic evaluations of prevention and care strategies in healthcare organisations and public health campaigns. His research spans infectious disease management, multimorbidity, primary care, and nursing team organisation. He contributed to governmental reports on the cost-effectiveness and budget impact of prevention strategies for influenza, pneumococcus, and RSV. He is principal investigator on projects focused on model-based public health decision-making and work package leader in multi-centric clinical trials. His commitment to public health and policy continues through his roles as a member of the Young Academy of Belgium and Chair of the User Council of the Flemish Supercomputer Centre.
